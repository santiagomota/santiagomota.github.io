This page brings together a selection of my public projects. They range from practical data applications to technical presentations and curated resources. The emphasis is on open data, reproducibility and useful results.

Featured projects

Wildfire monitoring in Spain

Visor Fuego
Live application · Source code

An interactive viewer of wildfire risk and active fires in Spain. The map is updated twice a day, bringing together information useful for monitoring changing fire conditions.

Focus: geospatial visualization · environmental data · automated updates
